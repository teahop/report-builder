Midway through sixth grade, the family explored a dyslexia specialty school, Pinecrest Gateway Academy. Despite initial school refusal and poor experiences with previous homeschool classes, Caleb Whitfield expressed interest after a two-hour visit and started attending two days later. He has been at Pinecrest Gateway since the second semester of sixth grade. He struggled more behaviorally in seventh grade and has matured and many had less referrals to the office this year than last.

**Language Arts Instruction: Mother is a certified teacher. Caleb Whitfield and his mother initially worked on the Foundation in Sounds and Barton Reading programs. He also had sessions with a tutor for Barton Reading, but this was not a good fit, as Caleb Whitfield often refused to attend despite having a good rapport with the tutor. The family then transitioned to the Rooted in Language program, which uses a SPEECH to PRINT approach. This method, supported by educator training and guidance, proved effective for Caleb Whitfield, providing explicit language arts instruction tailored to struggling learners and those with dyslexia.**

**Current Schooling at Pinecrest Gateway Academy: Pinecrest Gateway Academy (PGA) Pinecrest Gateway Academy is WASC Accredited private school that offers Structured Literacy Programs designed to help students with language-based learning disabilities, dysgraphia, dyscalculia, and dyslexia. Attending Pinecrest Gateway Academy is a significant daily intervention for Caleb Whitfield. The specialized non-public agency school offers focused, explicit multisensory instruction in phonics and reading skills, as well as specialized math instruction. All subjects are taught by educators informed about dyslexia, providing a supportive and understanding learning environment for Caleb Whitfield. Class size is extremely small with a 5 to1 student/teacher ratio.**
